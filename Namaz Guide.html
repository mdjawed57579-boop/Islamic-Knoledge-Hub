Namaz Guide — Sahih Hadees ke Mutabiq

Yahaan simple aur seedha tareeqa diya gaya hai jo Sahih Bukhaari aur Sahih Muslim me mazkoor hadees par mabni hai. (Niyat dil mein hoti hai — zubaan se padhne ki zaroorat nahin.)[1]

1. Niyat (Irada)

• Niyat dil (qalb) mein karo — zubaan se padhna zaroori nahi. Yani: jis namaz ki rakat padhna hai, uska irada dil mein karo. [1]

2. Takbeer-e-Tahreema (Shuru)

• Dono haath uthao qibla ki taraf (kaan/shoulder tak) aur bolo: Allahu Akbar.
• Nabi ﷺ ne namaz shuru karne par haath uthate the. [3]

3. Qiyam (Khade Hona) — Haath kahan rakhne

• Haath seene par rakho — right hand over left (right haath left ke upar). Ye Sunnah mashhoora tareeqa hai. [4]

4. Surah Al-Fatihah

• Har rakat mein Surah Al-Fatihah parhna zaroori (lazmi) hai. Agar koi Fatihah padh nahi sakta to jo parh sake woh padhe. [2]

5. Ruku

• Ruku mein jhuk kar haath ghutnon par rakhna, peeth seedhi rakhna aur kam az kam teen dafa kehna: Subḥāna Rabbiyal-ʿAẓīm.
• Ruku se uth kar “Sami'Allahu liman ḥamidah” aur phir “Rabbana wa lakal ḥamd” bolna. [3]

6. Sajdah

• Maatha, naak, dono haath, dono ghutne aur dono paon ki ungliyon par sajdah karo. Har sajdah mein kam az kam teen dafa bolo: Subḥāna Rabbiyal-Aʿlā.
• Sajdah ke darmiyan baith kar chhota sa tashahud (Rabbighfir li) kehna sunnat hai.

7. Tashahhud, Durood aur Salaam

• Aakhir baithne (qa'dah) mein Tashahhud padhna: At-tahiyyatu lillahi... (jaise Sahih Bukhari aur Muslim ka matn).
• Phir Durood (salawat) padho aur phir dono taraf salam do: Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah — pehle right, phir left. [2]

Chhoti se summary (quick):
Niyat (dil mein) → Takbeer (haath uthao) → Qiyam (Fatihah + surah) → Ruku (Subhana...) → Qawmah (Sami'a...) → Sajdah (Subhana...) → Do rakat → Tashahhud → Durood → Salaam.

References (Sahih Bukhari & Sahih Muslim):

[3] — Raising hands at beginning of prayer, at bowing, and saying “Samiʿa” — Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim (reports). 2
